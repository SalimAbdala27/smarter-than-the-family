PICTURE ROUND
Drop images in here and they become typed-answer quiz questions.

  Name the file after the answer:      Fish and chips.jpg
  Extra accepted answers in brackets:  Ed Sheeran (Sheeran, Ed).jpg

The folder decides the question:
  food/            → "Name this food"
  famous-faces/    → "Who is this?"   (also: people, celebs, artists, singers)
  logos/           → "Whose logo is this?"
  places/          → "Where is this?"
  flags/           → "Which country's flag is this?"
  cars/            → "Name this car"
  anything else    → "What is this?"

"Picture Round" appears in the Pub Quiz genres once there's at least one image.
